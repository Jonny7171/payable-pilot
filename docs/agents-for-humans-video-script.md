# PayablePilot video script for Agents for Humans

Target length: 90 seconds. The video works without narration, but the lines
below can also be read in a natural voice over the matching scenes.

## 0:00 to 0:12

Show the title card.

Say:

"This is PayablePilot. It clears routine invoice packets and brings a person in
only when a payment could be wrong."

## 0:12 to 0:26

Show the problem card.

Say:

"It is for small finance teams that lose time checking clean invoices but still
need a safe way to stop exceptions."

## 0:26 to 0:44

Show the live queue and the two packets.

Say:

"The Strands agent lists the queue, inspects each three-way match, and chooses
the next tool. One packet agrees and clears. The other does not."

## 0:44 to 1:02

Show the exception and architecture.

Say:

"Eight monitor arms were ordered at ninety-four dollars and invoiced at one
hundred nineteen. TypeScript calculates the two-hundred-dollar difference. The
agent can gather evidence and queue the review, but it cannot approve payment."

## 1:02 to 1:20

Show the resolved state.

Say:

"A person requests the credit, so the invoice stays on hold. The source values,
tool calls, and final choice remain visible in the run evidence."

## 1:20 to 1:30

Show the proof card.

Say:

"PayablePilot is public, reproducible, and covered by fourteen tests. Strands
handles the workflow. Code owns the money. A person owns the exception."

## Recording notes

- Do not claim that the AgentCore-compatible runtime is deployed to AWS.
- Do not call the deterministic proof model Bedrock or a live LLM.
- Keep the invoice records labelled as fictional.
- Small pauses will sound better than reading quickly.
